FAQ — UAE / Dubai

Can I convert visit visa to work in UAE?

Sometimes via proper employer process — not a DIY loophole. Follow legal channels.

Do I need degree attestation for Dubai jobs?

Often yes (MOFA / embassy / UAE attestation chain). SK Immigration handles Musadaqa & related attestation.
