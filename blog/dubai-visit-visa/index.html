UAE / Dubai: Visit & Work Pathways

Updated guide by SK Immigration Services · Serving applicants from any country · Embassies make final decisions

Dubai/UAE is popular for visit visas, skilled employment and regional business travel. Visit files need clear purpose, funds, and return ties. Work routes require employer sponsorship.

Who this destination suits

  • Fast regional travel hub
  • Employment opportunities in selected sectors
  • Visit + business options
  • Attestation often required for long-term files

Typical documents checklist

  • Passport validity
  • Invitation or hotel + itinerary
  • Funds / sponsor documents
  • Employment contract for work cases
  • Attested documents when required

Requirements change by university, embassy and nationality. Your SK Immigration consultant will give a tailored list.
