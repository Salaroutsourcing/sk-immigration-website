Australia: Student Visa Guide

Updated guide by SK Immigration Services · Serving applicants from any country · Embassies make final decisions

Australia offers high-quality education and temporary graduate work options for eligible students. Costs are higher; GTE/genuine student assessments and financial capacity are critical.

Who this destination suits

  • High education quality
  • Post-study work for eligible graduates
  • English environment
  • Strong global recognition

Typical documents checklist

  • COE / offer
  • Genuine student evidence
  • Funds evidence
  • English test as required
  • OSHC health cover

Requirements change by university, embassy and nationality. Your SK Immigration consultant will give a tailored list.
