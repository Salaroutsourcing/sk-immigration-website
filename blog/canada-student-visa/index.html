Canada: Study Permit Guide

Updated guide by SK Immigration Services · Serving applicants from any country · Embassies make final decisions

Canada is popular for study permits with co-op options and long-term PR planning. Approval depends on genuine student intent, acceptance letter, proof of funds, ties, and biometrics. Rules change — always verify current IRCC requirements.

Who this destination suits

  • Quality education system
  • Work options during/after study for eligible students
  • Pathway planning toward PR for some profiles
  • Diverse campuses

Typical documents checklist

  • Letter of acceptance from DLI
  • Proof of funds / GIC where applicable
  • SOP explaining study purpose
  • Passport, biometrics, medical if required
  • PAL/provincial attestation where applicable

Requirements change by university, embassy and nationality. Your SK Immigration consultant will give a tailored list.
