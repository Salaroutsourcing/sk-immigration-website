FAQ — Germany

Is Germany tuition-free for international students?

Yes, most public universities charge no tuition — students only pay a nominal semester contribution (€150–€350) which covers public transport passes.

Can I do Ausbildung without fluent German?

Employers typically require A2–B1 German to start. You can learn German while your file and CV are matched with approved employers.

What is a Sperrkonto?

A blocked bank account proving you have living expense funds for your first study year. You withdraw money monthly upon arrival in Germany.
