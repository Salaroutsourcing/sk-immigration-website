Portugal: Student & Work Guide

Updated guide by SK Immigration Services · Serving applicants from any country · Embassies make final decisions

Portugal is popular for milder living costs (vs some Western EU cities), English programs, and EU access after eligible study. Visa success still depends on genuine intent, funds, and complete documentation.

Who this destination suits

  • Welcoming student cities
  • Growing English programs
  • EU mobility potential
  • Balanced lifestyle costs

Typical documents checklist

  • Acceptance letter
  • Proof of means of subsistence
  • Accommodation evidence
  • Passport and insurance
  • Academic records

Requirements change by university, embassy and nationality. Your SK Immigration consultant will give a tailored list.
