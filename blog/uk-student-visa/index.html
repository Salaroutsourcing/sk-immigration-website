United Kingdom: Student & Visit Visa Guide

Updated guide by SK Immigration Services · Serving applicants from any country · Embassies make final decisions

The UK remains a top study destination for English-taught degrees. Applicants typically need a CAS from a licensed sponsor, proof of funds, English language evidence (often IELTS or approved alternative), and a clear study plan. Visit visas require strong ties to the home country.

Who this destination suits

  • World-ranked universities
  • Graduate Route for eligible graduates
  • English-speaking environment
  • Strong business and STEM programs

Typical documents checklist

  • CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies)
  • Financial evidence meeting UKVI rules
  • Passport, TB test where required
  • English language certificate (as required)
  • Credible SOP / interview readiness

Requirements change by university, embassy and nationality. Your SK Immigration consultant will give a tailored list.
