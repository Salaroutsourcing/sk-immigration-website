France: Student & Work Pathways

Updated guide by SK Immigration Services · Serving applicants from any country · Embassies make final decisions

France offers public and private university options, Campus France processes in many countries, and growing English-taught master's programs. Work-study combinations and long-stay student visas depend on nationality and case type.

Who this destination suits

  • Strong public university reputation
  • Cultural and academic diversity
  • Post-study options for some graduates
  • EU mobility after eligible status

Typical documents checklist

  • Campus France / university admission steps where required
  • Proof of funds and housing
  • Passport and academic file
  • Language evidence (French or English depending on program)
  • Visa appointment documents

Requirements change by university, embassy and nationality. Your SK Immigration consultant will give a tailored list.
