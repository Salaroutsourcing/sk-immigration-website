Frequently asked questions

How to get Canada work permit from Pakistan?

Most applicants need a qualifying job offer and LMIA (unless exempt), then apply to IRCC with biometrics/medicals. SK prepares documents.

Is Express Entry a work permit?

Express Entry is mainly permanent residence. Work permits are temporary. We explain which path fits your profile.

SK Canada work fee?

From PKR 80,000. IRCC fees separate. No guarantees.

Do I need IELTS for work permit?

Not always for the permit itself, but employers and PR pathways often need language scores.

Can students work in Canada?

Study permits have separate work-hour rules. Do not confuse with employer-specific work permits.

Who decides?

IRCC. SK prepares honest files only.
