Germany work permit / work visa from Pakistan

Germany is one of the strongest legal work pathways from Pakistan: skilled jobs, shortage occupations, and Ausbildung (earn-while-you-train). Success depends on a real employer, correct documents and honest language planning — not “guaranteed visa” agents.

Requirements

  • Valid job offer / employment contract or Ausbildung training contract from a German employer
  • Passport valid 6+ months with blank pages
  • Recognized qualifications or experience matching the role (recognition may apply)
  • German language level matching the job (often A2–B1; nursing/care usually higher)
  • Health insurance and clean civil documents
  • Proof you can enter legally via the correct national visa category

Germany work vs Ausbildung vs study — choose correctly

Do not mix pathways. A genuine job offer or Ausbildung contract is the core of most work files. If you only want a degree, use Germany study visa. If you want dual training with a training salary, start with Ausbildung Pakistan.

  • Language plan before you pay VFS (often A2–B1+)
  • Contract authenticity and employer details
  • Document attestation order for the destination
