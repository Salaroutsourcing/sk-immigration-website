### GERMANY STUDY VISA

**Q: How to apply for Germany student visa from Pakistan?**
A: Step-by-step: 1) Get university admission (or Ausbildung offer). 2) Open a blocked account (€11,208+). 3) Get health insurance. 4) Book embassy appointment via VFS. 5) Submit documents. 6) Attend visa interview. Timeline: typically 4–7 months. Full guide: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/study-visa/germany-study-visa-pakistan/ — SK Immigration Services helps with the complete process.

**Q: What is Germany Ausbildung and can Pakistanis apply?**
A: Ausbildung is Germany's dual vocational training program — you work and study simultaneously, earning a salary (€600–€1,000/month) while training for 2–3 years. Yes, Pakistanis can apply. German language (B1/B2) is usually required. Full guide: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/ausbildung.html — SK Immigration Services specializes in Ausbildung applications.

**Q: Do I need IELTS for Germany student visa?**
A: Not always. Many German universities teach in German (no IELTS needed). For English-taught programs, IELTS or TOEFL is required. MOI (Medium of Instruction) letter can substitute for some programs. SK Immigration advises on your specific situation: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/blog/germany-student-visa/

**Q: What is the Germany blocked account amount for 2026?**
A: €11,208 (€934/month × 12 months) as of 2025/2026. Subject to change — verify with the German embassy. SK Immigration guides clients through blocked account opening: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/answers/blocked-account-germany

**Q: What are Germany study visa documents required for Pakistan?**
A: Passport, university admission/Ausbildung offer, IELTS/German certificate, blocked account proof, health insurance, SOP (Statement of Purpose), academic transcripts, CV, visa application form, VFS appointment confirmation. Full checklist: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/checklist.html?country=de

**Q: How to apply for UK student visa from Pakistan?**
A: 1) Get CAS from a licensed UK sponsor university. 2) Meet IELTS/SELT requirements. 3) Show funds (tuition + £1,023+/month maintenance). 4) Pay IHS (Immigration Health Surcharge). 5) Apply online + biometrics at VFS. Timeline: typically 3–6 weeks after application. Full guide: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/study-visa/uk-study-visa-pakistan/ — Contact SK Immigration: +92 304 5999859

**Q: What is CAS for UK student visa?**
A: CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) is a unique reference number from your licensed UK university sponsor, required before you can apply for a UK Student visa. SK Immigration helps you understand CAS requirements: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/answers/uk-student-visa-cas

**Q: Do I need IELTS for UK student visa?**
A: Usually yes — a Secure English Language Test (IELT Academic, PTE, TOEFL) accepted by UKVI is required unless you qualify for an exemption (e.g., prior UK study, certain nationalities). ### DOCUMENT SERVICES

**Q: What is MOFA attestation in Pakistan?**
A: MOFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) attestation is the final attestation step for documents used abroad. It follows HEC/Board/NADRA attestation. SK Immigration handles complete attestation chains: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/document-services/ — See: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/answers/apostille-vs-mofa-vs-musadaqa

**Q: What is Apostille in Pakistan?**
A: Pakistan joined the Hague Apostille Convention in 2022. MOFA now issues Apostille certificates instead of traditional attestation for countries in the Hague Convention. SK Immigration provides Apostille guidance: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/document-services/

**Q: What is HEC attestation?**
A: HEC (Higher Education Commission) attestation verifies Pakistani degrees and transcripts for use abroad. Required for most student visa applications. SK Immigration guides through the process: https://immigration.salaroutsourcing.com/document-services/
