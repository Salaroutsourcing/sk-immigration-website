The blocked account (Sperrkonto) is still the document German missions ask most Pakistani students to prove living funds. The **euro amount is not a SK Immigration fee** — it is a government/bank requirement that can change.

## What to do this week

1. Confirm the **current monthly rate × 12** on the German mission site, not on social media.
2. Keep the blocked-account confirmation letter with your admission, insurance, and appointment slip.
3. Do not transfer funds until the university letter and appointment plan are real.
