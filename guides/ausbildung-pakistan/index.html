Ausbildung Pakistan — Germany dual training

Quick answer: Ausbildung is Germany’s dual vocational training: you train with a company and vocational school while earning a training salary. It is not a tourist visa and not “easy Germany.” Language (often A2–B1+, higher for nursing) and a real training contract matter. German missions decide visas.

Who it may suit

  • Applicants ready to learn German seriously
  • Profiles targeting nursing, IT, trades, hospitality and other shortage fields
  • Students comparing Ausbildung vs full university degrees

Typical building blocks

  1. Language plan
  2. CV / motivation package
  3. Employer / training contract
  4. Visa file for the correct national category
