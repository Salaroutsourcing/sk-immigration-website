Process

Recruitment process

  1. Brief: Submit workforce requirement or quotation form
  2. Shortlist: We match trades and experience from the candidate database
  3. Screen: CV pack + optional interviews coordinated with your team
  4. Select: Employer confirms offers and contract terms
  5. Document: Attestation / medical / visa support as required by destination
  6. Deploy: Travel readiness checklist before departure
