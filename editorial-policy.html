Content is published by the SK Immigration Services study-visa desk in Satellite Town, Rawalpindi. We do not invent named “expert” credentials. When a page shows an author, it means the organization editorial desk unless a real staff byline is added later with that person’s consent.

Source priority

  1. Official government / embassy / immigration authority pages
  2. Official study portals (for example Study in Hungary, Study Australia, Campus France-linked guidance)
  3. University admissions pages for programme-specific language and fee rules
  4. VFS / appointment centre procedural pages
  5. Our case-handling experience (process coaching only — not a substitute for law)

Country pages link to an official links hub. If a fee or form number is uncertain, we say so instead of guessing.

What we never publish

  • Visa approval guarantees or fixed “success rate” marketing
  • Fake testimonials, fake reviews or fabricated statistics
  • Copied embassy text presented as our original guide
  • Doorway pages that only exist to host ads
  • Advice to hide refusals or submit false documents

Review dates

Guides show an “Updated” or “Reviewed” date when refreshed. Immigration rules change — always re-check the official links on the same page before you pay fees.

Tools vs advice

Our free tools (eligibility quiz, checklist, calculator, compare, CV builder) are planning aids. They do not approve visas and may simplify complex rules. Use them, then confirm on official sources and with a human consult if needed.

Corrections

If you find an outdated link or unclear statement, email us with the URL and the official source. We prioritise study-visa corrections that could cost students money.
