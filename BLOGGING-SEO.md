# Will posting blogs daily make them rank?

**Short answer:** Daily posting alone does **not** guarantee Google ranking. Quality + intent + structure beats volume.

## What Google (and AI) actually reward

| Helps ranking | Hurts / wastes time |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Answers a real question clearly | Thin 200-word posts every day |
| Unique facts, checklists, costs, steps | Copied AI fluff with no expertise |
| Good title = the question people ask | Keyword stuffing |
| Internal links to services / FAQ / contact | Orphan pages with no links |
| Updated dates + accurate info | Fake “100% visa” claims |
| Shares from Instagram/TikTok/YouTube | Duplicate posts across many URLs |

## Best publishing rhythm for SK Immigration

- **3–4 strong posts per week** beats forced daily posts
- Or **1 excellent country/FAQ article** every 2–3 days
- Each post should be **800–1,500+ words**, with:
  - Clear H1 question/title
  - H2 sections (requirements, cost, timeline, mistakes, next steps)
  - CTA to WhatsApp / free consult
  - Link to related country guide + eligibility quiz

## Titles that rank + get AI citations

Use question titles people type into ChatGPT/Google:

- Can I study in Germany with 55% marks?
- Do I need IELTS for Hungary student visa?
- Ausbildung nursing salary in Germany for foreigners
- Study in Poland without IELTS — realistic options
- Document attestation for UAE from Pakistan — steps

## Special way to rank faster

1. Publish on your site first (`/blog/.../`)
2. Make a **short TikTok + Instagram Reel + YouTube Short** summarizing the same tip
3. Caption link → your blog URL
4. Pin comment with WhatsApp number
5. After 48 hours → Google Search Console → **Request indexing**

This is how your socials + SEO reinforce each other.
