2. Why Pakistani Students Choose Australia

Australia remains a top destination for Pakistani students due to its globally recognized education system, safe and multicultural environment, and strong post-study work opportunities. Popular fields include information technology, healthcare, engineering, and business management.

3. Australia Student Visa Requirements for Pakistani Students

For most applicants applying from Pakistan, the core application file includes:

  • A valid Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from a CRICOS-registered institution.
  • A Genuine Student (GS) response to the questions in the application form, proving your intent to study temporarily.
  • Evidence of financial capacity to cover the first year of tuition, living costs (AUD 29,710), travel, and dependant costs (if applicable).
  • English language proficiency evidence (e.g., IELTS, PTE) meeting course and visa requirements.
  • Valid Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for your entire proposed stay.
  • A health examination and biometrics may be required after lodging.

4. Genuine Student (GS) Requirement: The Core of Your Application

The Genuine Student (GS) requirement replaced the Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) statement. It is now assessed through a series of mandatory questions in the online application form (ImmiAccount). The Department of Home Affairs will use your answers, along with your supporting documents, to assess your intentions.

The key questions you must answer are:

  1. Your current circumstances (ties to Pakistan, personal and economic situation).
  2. Why you chose this specific course and education provider.
  3. How completing the course will benefit your future career or personal goals.
  4. Any other relevant information the department should consider.
SK Immigration practical tip Do not write vague or generic answers. Be specific. For example, instead of saying "I want to study IT because it is a good career," explain *why this exact university's curriculum* and *this particular specialization* fit your past experience and future business plan in Pakistan. Provide evidence like job offer letters, family business details, or a clear career timeline.
Department of Home Affairs requirement Pakistan is currently classified at a higher "Evidence Level 3." This means you will generally need to provide full financial and English documentation with your application. Do not assume a lower-evidence standard applies. Check the official assessment levels.

5. Australia Student Visa Financial Requirements

You must prove you have enough money to support yourself for the first 12 months of your stay. The total you need to show is generally calculated as:

Tuition for your first year (from CoE) + Living Costs (AUD 29,710 for main student) + Travel Costs (approx. AUD 1,000-2,000) + OSHC costs + Dependant costs (if applicable).

Funds should be in a bank account for a recommended minimum of 3 months to be considered "seasoned."

SK Immigration practical tip Large, recent deposits are a major red flag. If you receive a large sum, keep clear records of its source (e.g., a property sale deed, a gift deed from parents, a business profit statement) to explain the transaction clearly. Do not submit unverifiable or fake bank statements.

6. English Language Requirement

You must demonstrate English proficiency, typically through a recognized test.

  • IELTS: A minimum overall score of 6.0 (with no band below 5.5) is common for many courses, but some may require 6.5 or higher.
  • PTE Academic: Acceptable and often faster. Equivalent scores are typically around 50-58.
  • TOEFL iBT: Also accepted.
  • Medium of Instruction (MOI): Some universities may accept evidence that your previous education was taught entirely in English.

Check the requirements for your specific university course, as it may set a higher standard than the base visa requirement.

7. OSHC and Health Examinations

Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is mandatory. It provides insurance for medical and hospital care in Australia. You can purchase it from one of the five approved providers: ahm, Bupa, Medibank, Allianz Care Australia, or nib. Cost is approximately AUD 623 – 806 per year for a single student.

After lodging your visa application, you will be asked to undertake a health examination (including a chest x-ray) with an approved panel physician in Pakistan, as you are a resident of a country with a TB risk. You must attend this appointment before the visa is processed.

8. Step-by-Step Process from Pakistan

  1. Course and Provider Shortlist: Choose a CRICOS-registered course and university that fits your academic and career background.
  2. Apply and Receive an Offer: Apply for admission. If successful, you will receive a Letter of Offer.
  3. Accept Offer and Get CoE: Accept the offer, pay the tuition deposit as required, and receive your electronic Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE). You cannot apply for the visa without a CoE.
  4. Prepare and Lodge Application: Gather all evidence (GS answers, financial evidence, English, OSHC). Create an ImmiAccount online and lodge your visa application. Pay the AUD 2,500 application fee.
  5. Biometrics and Health Exam: Follow instructions to book and attend a biometrics appointment (fingerprints and photo) and a health examination with an approved clinic in Pakistan.
  6. Wait for a Decision: The Department of Home Affairs will assess your application. Processing times vary and can take several weeks to a few months. You may be asked for additional information.
  7. Receive Decision and Prepare for Departure: If approved, you will receive your visa grant notification. Check all conditions and travel dates.

9. Common Australia Student Visa Mistakes

  • Generic GS responses: Writing vague, non-specific answers that don't connect the course to your personal story.
  • Unexplained or unseasoned funds: Not maintaining funds in the bank account for at least 3 months or failing to explain the source of large deposits.
  • Course hopping: Having a history of changing courses frequently without a clear, logical reason.
  • Ignoring health exam timing: Delaying the required health examination, which can significantly slow down processing.
  • Submitting incomplete documents: Failing to provide all required documents or submitting documents that don't meet the criteria (e.g., old bank statements).
Warning Never submit fabricated or falsified documents. The Department of Home Affairs conducts rigorous checks, and providing false information will lead to an automatic refusal and may result in a ban from applying for Australian visas in the future.

11. Frequently Asked Questions

How much bank balance is required for an Australian Student Visa from Pakistan?

You must show enough funds for the first 12 months of tuition (as shown on your CoE) + AUD 29,710 for living costs + approximately AUD 2,000 for travel + OSHC. If bringing family, add AUD 10,394 for a spouse and AUD 4,449 per child. Funds should ideally be “seasoned” (held consistently) in a bank account for 3–6 months.

Is IELTS required for an Australia study visa?

Not always. A score of IELTS 6.0–6.5 is common for university courses, but Pearson PTE, TOEFL iBT, or Cambridge C1 Advanced are also accepted. Some students can use a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter if their previous education was fully in English. Always check your specific course and university requirements.

What is the Genuine Student (GS) requirement for Australia?

It is a mandatory set of questions in the online visa application (ImmiAccount). You must prove you are a genuine student who intends to study in Australia. It replaced the old GTE statement from 23 March 2024. Each answer is limited to 150 words.

How long does an Australian student visa take from Pakistan?

Processing times vary. For complete applications from Pakistan, it commonly takes 4–8 weeks, but can range from a few weeks to several months depending on your institution’s priority status, document completeness, and any additional checks. Always lodge early.

Can I work on an Australia student visa?

Yes. You can work up to 48 hours per fortnight while your course is in session, and unlimited hours during scheduled course breaks. Master’s by research and PhD students have no work hour limits.

Can I bring my spouse or children?

Yes, if you can show additional funds (approx. AUD 10,394 for a spouse and AUD 4,449 per child). They must also have health insurance (OSHC) and meet health and character requirements. Your spouse can usually work full-time.

What are the post-study work options?

Most graduates who complete a bachelor’s or master’s degree are eligible for a Temporary Graduate Visa (subclass 485). This allows them to work in Australia for 2–4 years depending on the qualification and location of study.

Is a TB test required from Pakistan?

Yes, for stays longer than 6 months. You must undergo a health examination, including a chest x-ray, with a panel physician approved by the Australian government in Pakistan.

What is the current Australian student visa fee from Pakistan?

From 1 July 2026, the standard Subclass 500 visa application charge is AUD 2,500 for the primary applicant. Additional applicants (spouse/children) pay extra. The fee is non-refundable even if the visa is refused.

What documents are required for an Australian student visa from Pakistan?

Key documents include: valid passport, Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC), proof of funds (bank statements), English language test results, academic transcripts, Genuine Student (GS) answers, health examination results, and biometrics.

Do I need OSHC for an Australian student visa?

Yes. Overseas Student Health Cover is mandatory for the entire duration of your stay in Australia. You must arrange it before lodging your visa application.

How much study gap is acceptable for Australia from Pakistan?

There is no fixed limit. Gaps of 1–2 years are usually easy to justify. Gaps of 2–5 years (especially for master’s) are commonly accepted if explained properly with work experience, family responsibilities, or skill development. Longer gaps need strong supporting documents.

Can I apply for an Australian student visa without IELTS?

Yes, in some cases. You can use PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, Cambridge English, or a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from your previous institution if the entire course was taught in English. University entry requirements may still require a test.

What is the difference between GTE and Genuine Student (GS)?

GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) was the old requirement. From 23 March 2024 it was replaced by the Genuine Student (GS) requirement. Instead of writing a long statement, you now answer structured questions (maximum 150 words each) inside the ImmiAccount form.

Can my parents sponsor me for an Australian student visa?

Yes. Parents can act as financial sponsors. You will need to provide their bank statements, income proof, relationship evidence (e.g. birth certificate or family registration), and a sponsorship letter explaining the source of funds.

Is education loan accepted as proof of funds for Australia?

Yes. Education loans from recognised banks or financial institutions are accepted as proof of funds, provided the loan is approved and the disbursement letter clearly shows the amount available for your studies.

What are the most common reasons for Australian student visa refusal from Pakistan?

The most common reasons include: weak Genuine Student (GS) responses, insufficient or poorly documented funds, course choice that does not logically match previous education or career goals, incomplete documents, and issues with OSHC coverage.

Can I change my course or university after getting the student visa?

Yes, but you must meet certain conditions. You generally need to complete at least 6 months of your principal course before changing providers, and you must update the Department of Home Affairs and maintain a valid CoE.

Do I need biometrics for an Australian student visa from Pakistan?

Yes. Most applicants from Pakistan are required to provide biometrics (fingerprints and photo) at an Australian Visa Application Centre (AVAC) after lodging the application.

How early should I apply for the Australian student visa?

It is recommended to apply as soon as you receive your CoE. For Pakistani applicants, lodging 8–12 weeks before the course start date is safer, especially during peak intake periods.

Can I apply for permanent residency after studying in Australia?

Yes. Completing an eligible qualification can open pathways such as the Temporary Graduate Visa (485), skilled migration visas, or employer-sponsored visas. Permanent residency is not guaranteed and depends on your occupation, points, and current immigration rules.

Is a police character certificate required?

It is not always required at the time of application, but the Department of Home Affairs may request a police clearance certificate (or character documents) during processing, especially if you have lived in other countries or if there are any concerns.

What happens if my Australian student visa is refused?

You will receive a refusal letter explaining the reasons. You may be able to reapply with stronger documents and better GS answers, or in some cases request a review (Merits Review) if you are eligible. The visa fee is not refundable.

Can I study two courses on one student visa?

Yes. Package courses (e.g. English + main degree, or diploma + bachelor’s) are common. You must have CoEs for all courses and the total duration must be covered by your visa and OSHC.

Do I need to show funds for the full course duration?

No. You only need to show funds for the first 12 months (or the full course length if it is shorter than 12 months). However, you must still convince the officer that you can support yourself for the entire stay.
