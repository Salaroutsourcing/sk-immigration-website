SK Immigration Services (SK Consultant, CUIN 0304985) prepares study, visit, work and Saudi files from Rawalpindi with WhatsApp support nationwide. Embassies and consulates decide every visa. We do not sell guarantees. Read how to verify us before paying any fee.

Five-step process (all services)

  1. Free consult — WhatsApp or walk-in. We ask marks, gaps, budget, passport history and refusals.
  2. Honest fit check — we shortlist real options or say “not yet” (language, funds, marks).
  3. Checklist & scope — country checklist, SK fee vs official fees in writing where requested.
  4. Document packaging — cover letters, funds narrative, attestation order, appointment sequencing.
  5. Submission support — you lodge on official portals; we coach interviews and track steps. Outcome is decided by authorities.
