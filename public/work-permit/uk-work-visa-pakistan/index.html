United Kingdom work permit / work visa from Pakistan

The UK is highly regulated: only Home Office licensed sponsors can issue CoS. We never invent sponsorships.

Requirements

  • Certificate of Sponsorship from a licensed UK sponsor
  • Job meeting skill and salary thresholds for the route
  • English language at required CEFR level
  • TB test for Pakistan residents when required
  • Passport, TB certificate, and online UKVI application

Frequently asked questions

How to get UK Skilled Worker visa from Pakistan?

A licensed UK sponsor issues a CoS; you apply on GOV.UK with English, TB and identity documents. SK prepares the file; UKVI decides.

Can SK find me a UK sponsor?

We help with CV packaging and realistic guidance. Sponsorship must come from a real licensed employer — we do not sell fake CoS.

SK UK work fee?

From PKR 80,000. UKVI/IHS/TB fees separate.

Do I need IELTS for UK work?

Usually an approved English test at the level required by the visa route (unless exempt).

Graduate route vs Skilled Worker?

Graduate Route is for eligible UK graduates. Skilled Worker needs sponsorship. Different paths.

Visa guarantee?

Never. UKVI decides.
