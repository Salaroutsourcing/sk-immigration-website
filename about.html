Our story

SK Immigration Services is the immigration and study-visa brand of Salar Outsourcing. We help students and professionals navigate student visas, visit visas, Schengen work permits, Germany Ausbildung, complete Saudi work visa processing and document attestation.

We focus on realistic country matching, clean documentation and clear communication — never false “100% visa” promises. Embassies make final decisions; we prepare strong files.

Registration: SK Immigration is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP), Government of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. We partner with licensed Overseas Employment Promoters (OEPs); partner OEP licence NO/1061.
