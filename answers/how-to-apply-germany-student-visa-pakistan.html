How to apply for Germany student visa from Pakistan step by step?

Answer: From Pakistan, the Germany student path is usually: profile → university or Ausbildung contract → funds proof (often Sperrkonto) → complete document file → national visa appointment → decision. Timelines often run several months. German missions decide; SK Immigration prepares honest files.

Step-by-step

  1. Profile & pathway: Degree vs language + degree vs Ausbildung. Take our eligibility quiz.
  2. Shortlist: Programs or employers that match marks, language and budget.
  3. Admission or training contract: Collect offer / contract letters that missions accept.
  4. Funds: Open blocked account if required — Sperrkonto guide.
  5. Documents: Academics, translations, insurance, forms — use Germany checklist.
  6. Appointment: Book via the channel your mission uses (often VFS). See Germany appointment.
  7. Interview / biometrics & wait: Answer consistently with your SOP and funds story.
  8. After decision: Travel and residence registration steps as instructed.

Documents you should expect

Passport, photos, admission/contract, academics, funds proof, insurance, motivation letter/CV, application forms, and any APS or attestation steps that apply to your nationality/case. Confirm on German official links.

Realistic timeline

Many complete files still take about 4–7 months end-to-end when you include language, admission and appointment queues. Peak seasons run longer.

APS, attestation and translations

Depending on your profile and mission instructions, you may need APS, HEC/IBCC steps, or sworn translations. Wrong order wastes months. SK Immigration maps the chain before you pay stamp fees — see MOFA attestation and Germany official pages.

Language plan that missions respect

If your offer is German-taught or Ausbildung, show a concrete language pathway (current level → target level → test dates). Vague “I will learn German later” answers fail interviews.

After you land

Register address, extend residence as required, and keep health insurance continuous. Your visa vignette is only the start of German residence compliance.

Cost planning beyond the blocked account

Budget for visa fees, travel, first-month rent deposits, semester contribution, and health insurance — not only the Sperrkonto balance. Use the Germany cost calculator for a planning range, then verify live prices.
