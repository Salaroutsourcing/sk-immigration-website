How do I apply for a Germany student visa from Pakistan?

Prepared by SK Immigration Services (SMC-Private) Limited · CUIN 0304985 · Rawalpindi · Last review 2026-07-30

Answer: Apply by choosing the correct pathway (degree vs Ausbildung), securing admission or a training contract, proving funds the German way, completing attestation/APS steps your case needs, then filing a national visa through the mission/VFS with a complete set — not a short Schengen tourist form.

Step-by-step from Pakistan

  1. Profile choice — degree / Studienkolleg / Ausbildung (language and marks decide)
  2. Shortlist — real universities or employers; avoid guarantee brokers
  3. Admission or contract — written offer that matches your language proof
  4. Funds — Sperrkonto or Ausbildung salary proof (blocked account guide)
  5. Documents — academics, translations, APS/attestation when required
  6. Appointment — book only when the file is complete
  7. Biometrics / interview — answers must match papers
  8. Decision + travel — enrol and activate accounts after arrival rules

National visa vs Schengen visit

Long study/Ausbildung stays use a German national visa (D) process. A 15–90 day Schengen visit sticker is the wrong tool for a degree. See visit vs student.

Document kit (typical — confirm for your case)

  • Passport, photos, forms
  • Admission or Ausbildung contract
  • Funds proof
  • Academics + language certificates
  • Insurance meeting entry rules
  • APS / attestation papers when applicable

How long it takes

Many complete timelines run 4–7 months from shortlist to decision. Language gaps and peak seasons add time.

Choosing degree vs Ausbildung before forms

If your German is below the Ausbildung workplace level, forcing a nursing Ausbildung file wastes months. If your marks do not support a competitive English Master, a “any university admission” PDF will not save the visa. SK Immigration’s free consult exists to stop the wrong first payment.

APS and academics — plan early

Some pathways require APS or specific attestation orders for Pakistani credentials. Start document legalization while applications run — not after you receive an appointment SMS. Cross-check attestation types and German embassy notices via official links.

Motivation letter that survives reading

  • Name the university, city and program modules
  • Connect prior subjects/work to the degree or training
  • Explain funding source in one clear paragraph
  • Avoid copy-paste “Germany is rich in culture” essays

Appointment day kit

  1. Passport + copies as listed
  2. Admission/contract + funds confirmation
  3. Insurance evidence meeting rules
  4. Academic set + translations
  5. Forms signed consistently with online data

After refusal or delay

If refused, read refusal reasons and what next. If delayed, do not open duplicate blocked accounts “just in case” without advice — you can freeze cash unnecessarily.

Working while preparing the visa

Part-time job hopes in Germany should not be your funding plan on paper. Missions expect lawful living funds first. After arrival, student work rights depend on the permit — do not promise yourself illegal work to “make the blocked account.”

Family accompaniment

Bringing dependants later is a separate, heavier process. Most first-time applicants from Pakistan file solo. Discuss family timing only after the main permit logic is solid.

FAQ

How do I apply for a Germany student visa from Pakistan?

Secure admission or Ausbildung contract, prove funds correctly, complete required attestation/APS, then file a national visa via mission/VFS with a complete set.

Can I apply without blocked account?

Some pathways use alternatives or Ausbildung salary proof. Do not invent workarounds — verify official rules.

How long does Germany student visa take from Pakistan?

Often 4–7 months end-to-end; incomplete files take longer.
