Can I apply for a student visa with a 3–5 year study gap?

Answer: Often yes if the Statement of Purpose explains the gap honestly (work, family, skills). Hungary, Poland and Czech Republic are frequently gap-friendlier. Gaps above 5 years need stronger justification.
