How to Choose a Reliable Immigration Consultant in Pakistan

Pakistan has hundreds of immigration consultants — choosing the wrong one can cost you money, time, and your visa application. Here are the key checks:

  1. Verify SECP registration. A legitimate company should be registered. Check at leap.secp.gov.pk.
  2. No visa guarantee = honest consultant. Embassies and consulates make ALL visa decisions. Any consultant promising "100% visa" is lying.
  3. Transparent, documented fees. All charges should be in writing before you pay anything.
  4. Physical office with verifiable address. Avoid consultants with only WhatsApp numbers and no office.
  5. Matching contact information. The phone number on Google Business, website, and WhatsApp should be the same.
