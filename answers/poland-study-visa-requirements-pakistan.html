What are Poland study visa requirements from Pakistan?

Answer: Core Poland study requirements from Pakistan typically include a genuine admission/offer, proof of funds (Tuition + living for the requested period), identity documents, academics (with attestation/translation when asked), and language evidence (Many English programs; IELTS or university English test / MOI sometimes). Exact lists change — verify the mission checklist.

Document checklist (typical)

  • Valid passport + photos
  • Admission / offer letter from a recognized Poland institution
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Proof of funds / sponsor documents
  • Language certificates as required
  • Health insurance meeting mission rules
  • Completed application forms + fee receipts
  • SOP / motivation letter and CV where asked
