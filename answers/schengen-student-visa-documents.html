What documents are needed for a Schengen student visa?

Answer: Typically: passport, offer letter, proof of funds, insurance, accommodation, academic docs, SOP/CV, photos, form + fee. Country extras apply (Germany blocked account, Italy Dichiarazione di Valore, France Campus France).
