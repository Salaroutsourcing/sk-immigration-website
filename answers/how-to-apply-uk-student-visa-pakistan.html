How to apply for UK student visa from Pakistan step by step?

Answer: The UK route is CAS-first: get an offer from a licensed Student sponsor, receive a CAS, meet funds and English/TB rules, apply on GOV.UK, then complete biometrics (usually via VFS in Pakistan). UKVI decides the visa.

Step-by-step

  1. Choose a licensed sponsor and secure an offer.
  2. Meet conditions (deposit, documents, English as required).
  3. Receive CAS — do not apply before it is issued.
  4. Arrange funds to match GOV.UK maintenance rules and CAS tuition figures.
  5. TB test from an approved clinic if required for your case.
  6. Online application + IHS/fees on GOV.UK.
  7. Biometrics appointment — UK appointment guide.
  8. Decision & travel per vignette/eVisa instructions.

Documents checklist (high level)

  • Passport, CAS, offer letter
  • Funds evidence with correct day-count
  • English evidence if not exempt
  • TB certificate when required
  • Academic transcripts as the sponsor/UKVI expect

Timeline

Plan for offer + CAS first (weeks to months), then visa processing per published UKVI times. Peak intakes fill fast — do not leave funds or TB to the last week.

Official sources to verify

Rules change by nationality and intake. Always confirm on government pages before you pay fees. Browse all country official links.

IHS and fee planning

Budget for the visa fee and Immigration Health Surcharge in addition to tuition deposits and VFS charges. Families underestimate IHS — confirm current GOV.UK amounts before CAS deposits drain your maintenance buffer.

Credibility tips for Pakistani applicants

  • Know why this course beats studying the same field in Pakistan
  • Explain gaps with evidence (work letters, not excuses)
  • Keep social media / application stories consistent
  • Do not submit conflicting bank accounts across forms

Using VFS in Pakistan

Most biometrics happen through VFS. Arrive with the exact document set your checklist names, in the order staff expect. Optional paid VFS services do not improve UKVI approval odds — do not confuse convenience with influence.

Track application status only through official UKVI/VFS channels. SK Immigration can help you interpret requests for information, but we cannot “speed up” UKVI decisions.

Linking CAS, funds and travel dates

Your intended arrival should match CAS course start rules. Arriving far too early or without accommodation plans can create questions. Align flight deposits with visa decision risk — prefer flexible tickets when possible.
