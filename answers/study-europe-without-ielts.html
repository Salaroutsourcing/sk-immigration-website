Can I study in Europe without IELTS?

Answer: Sometimes. Many universities accept medium-of-instruction (MOI) letters, internal English tests, or interviews — especially in Hungary, Poland, Czech Republic, Malta, Cyprus and some German programs. UK/Ireland/Australia usually need secure English tests.
