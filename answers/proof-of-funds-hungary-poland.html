How much proof of funds for Hungary or Poland student visa?

Answer: Show enough money for remaining tuition + living costs for the period your mission lists. Numbers change by year and city — do not trust fixed Facebook screenshots. SK Immigration Services (SK Consultant) reviews statements case by case before you book VFS.

What “proof of funds” usually means

  • Student / sponsor bank statements with a clear trail
  • Tuition payment receipts or scholarship letters
  • Sometimes a sponsor affidavit + relationship proof
  • Living-cost coverage matching housing plans
