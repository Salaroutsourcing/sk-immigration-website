What is a Germany blocked account (Sperrkonto)?

Prepared by SK Immigration Services (SMC-Private) Limited · CUIN 0304985 · Rawalpindi · Last review 2026-07-30

Answer: A blocked account (Sperrkonto) is a German bank product that proves you can cover living costs for many national study visas. You deposit a required annual amount; monthly withdrawals are capped. The figure changes — verify current German mission guidance before opening. Ausbildung applicants usually prove funding via the training salary instead.

Why German missions ask for it

For university / language+study pathways, missions need evidence you will not become a public burden. A Sperrkonto is the standard tool for many nationalities, including applicants from Pakistan. Scholarships or formal obligations by a German resident can replace it only under strict rules.

Opening steps (high level)

  1. Confirm your visa category and current required amount on official guidance
  2. Choose a provider that issues confirmation letters German missions accept
  3. Transfer funds through a traceable channel; keep SWIFT/receipts
  4. Receive blocking confirmation for the visa file
  5. After arrival, activate and withdraw monthly within rules

Mistakes Pakistani students make

  • Opening an account for last year’s amount
  • Using a provider the mission rejects
  • Paying university deposits before knowing if Sperrkonto is even the right path
  • Borrowing funds that bounce after the confirmation letter

Official verification

Always re-check amounts and accepted providers via German mission / DAAD / Make it in Germany links. SK Immigration sequences your file so you do not open the wrong product. Related: Germany study visa Pakistan.

Who needs a Sperrkonto — and who might not

  • Usually yes: degree students and many language+study pathways showing living funds the classic way
  • Often different: Ausbildung trainees proving salary in a training contract
  • Sometimes alternative: formal obligation (Verpflichtungserklärung) or scholarship letters that missions accept — rare and document-heavy

SK Immigration confirms the category before you wire life savings to a provider that does not match your visa type.

Timing relative to admission

Do not open a blocked account the day a random agent demands a deposit. Ideal sequence: realistic admission/contract → confirm current amount on official guidance → open provider → receive confirmation letter → book national-visa appointment. Opening too early locks money while your offer is still uncertain; opening too late misses appointment slots.

Transfer tips from Pakistan

  • Use banking channels that produce clear SWIFT / remittance evidence
  • Name on the German account confirmation must align with passport naming
  • Keep every receipt for the visa file and for activation after arrival
  • Ask the provider how confirmation letters are issued for Islamabad / visa centre use

After you arrive in Germany

Activation rules differ by provider. Plan address registration and unblocking steps so you can withdraw the monthly living amount legally. Running out of accessible funds in month one is an avoidable crisis.

Provider shopping checklist

  • Does the German mission currently accept this provider’s confirmation format?
  • What are opening fees, monthly fees and early-closure rules?
  • How fast is the confirmation letter issued for visa filing from Pakistan?
  • Can the account holder name match your passport exactly?

SK Immigration does not take a secret commission to push a single bank. We help you avoid providers that create unusable letters for your visa category.

Linking Sperrkonto to the rest of the Germany file

Blocked-account proof must match the same person, intake and pathway as the admission letter. Mixing an Ausbildung contract with a student Sperrkonto “just in case” confuses officers. Keep one coherent story across forms, SOP and bank letters.

What is a Germany blocked account?

How much do I need in a Sperrkonto?

The required amount changes. Verify current embassy guidance before opening an account.

Usually they show salary via the training contract instead of a classic student Sperrkonto.
