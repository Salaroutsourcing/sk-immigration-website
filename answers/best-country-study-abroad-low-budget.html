Answer: “Cheapest” is not “best.” Lower total cost often means public European tuition + careful living budgets (e.g. parts of Central/Eastern Europe, Germany public routes) or Asia (Malaysia/Turkey) — but deposits, attestation, insurance and refusals can erase savings. Shortlist by total cost of attendance, not brochure tuition alone.
