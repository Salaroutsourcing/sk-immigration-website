Can I study in Slovakia with low marks?

Answer: Sometimes — but “low marks + guaranteed visa” offers are a red flag. For Slovakia (Schengen / EU), universities set academic bars; embassies check funds, intent and documents. SK Immigration maps realistic schools/pathways for ~50–65% profiles when the rest of the file is strong.

What “low marks” usually means in practice

Below ~60% intermediate/bachelor averages limit elite public universities, but some private institutions, pathway/foundation routes, or applied programs in Slovakia may still review your file — case by case.

Slovakia-specific reality check

Often compared with Hungary/Czech for mid-budget plans. Language: English or Slovak; IELTS / university English as required. Funds: Living + tuition per Slovak mission checklist.

What still must be strong

What will not work
