Visit visa vs student visa — what’s the difference?

Answer: Visit visas are short stays for tourism/family/business and forbid study/work beyond narrow rules. Student visas allow full programs with residence permits. Never enter on a visit visa intending to study long-term — that risks refusal/bans.
