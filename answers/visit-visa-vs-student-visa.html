Visit visa vs student visa — what’s the difference?

Answer: A visit visa is for a temporary trip (tourism, family, business) with limited or no study/work rights. A student visa is for an admitted program with living-cost proof and residence rules. From Pakistan, never use a visitor story to hide long-term study plans — embassies treat that as credibility failure.

Refusal risks of mixing routes

Visitor applications that look like study intent (university emails, long “holiday” with no return plan) fail often. Student applications with fake visit history or unexplained funds also fail. SK Immigration separates the stories on purpose.

Quick decision rule

If you will sit in a full degree or CAS program, choose student. If you will return after a short trip, choose visit. If you are unsure, take the eligibility quiz or WhatsApp SK before paying any agent deposit.
