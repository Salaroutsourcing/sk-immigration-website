What are Schengen visit visa requirements?

Answer: A Schengen visit visa (usually Type C) lets you visit the Schengen Area for short stays (tourism, family, business) — typically up to 90 days in 180 days. From Pakistan you normally apply via VFS/the mission of the country that is your main destination (or first entry if unclear). Requirements include passport, forms, photos, travel medical insurance, funds, itinerary/accommodation, and strong home ties.

Core document set (typical)

  • Valid passport with blank pages and recent photos
  • Completed application + appointment/biometrics
  • Travel medical insurance meeting Schengen minimums
  • Flight reservation / travel plan (follow mission rules on tickets)
  • Hotel bookings or host invitation + host ID/status docs
  • Bank statements and employment/business proof
  • Evidence you will return (job, family, property, studies)

Which country processes your file?

Apply to the country of main destination (where you spend most nights). If equal, the country of first entry often applies. Applying to the “easiest” mission while clearly travelling elsewhere is a credibility risk.

Funds and ties (why Pakistanis get refused)

Visit visas are refused when officers doubt return intent or financing. Thin bank history, unpaid leave stories without proof, or tourist plans that look like immigration attempts fail. See visit refusal reasons.

Visit vs study

Do not use a visit visa as a shortcut to study or work. Wrong purpose is a serious credibility hit for future applications. Compare visit vs student visa.

Insurance and itinerary tips

Buy Schengen-compliant travel medical insurance for the full stay with the minimum coverage your mission requires. Make sure names and dates match the application. Hotel cancellations and flexible bookings are fine when the mission allows reservations instead of fully paid tickets — follow that mission’s PDF checklist, not social media advice.

Business vs tourism visit

Business visitors need invitation letters, company registration excerpts, and a clear meeting purpose. Tourism files need a day-by-day plan that matches funds and leave letters. Mixing purposes without documents looks evasive.

After you get the visa

Enter through the country that issued the visa when practical, respect the sticker dates, and never overstay. Overstays damage future UK, Canada, USA and Schengen applications.

Multi-entry vs single-entry

First-time applicants from Pakistan often receive single-entry visas even when they asked for multi-entry. That is normal. Build a clean travel history with on-time exits before expecting longer multi-entry stickers. Never overstay to “use” unused days.

If your trip spans several Schengen countries, show a coherent route and enough funds for the whole loop — not only the first city hotel.
