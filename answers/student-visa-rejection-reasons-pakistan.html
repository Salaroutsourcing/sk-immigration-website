Student visa rejection reasons (Pakistan applicants)

SK Immigration Services (SMC-Private) Limited · CUIN 0304985 · Rawalpindi · Embassies decide visas

Answer: Most student visa refusals we see from Pakistani files cluster around funds credibility, unclear study purpose, incomplete or inconsistent documents, and language evidence that does not match the offer. The refusal letter is the authoritative list of reasons for your case — read it line by line before paying for a re-file.

What to do in the first 7 days after a refusal

  1. Save the full refusal PDF and note every stated reason.
  2. Stop paying anyone who promises a “guaranteed” reverse.
  3. Check whether the university offer is still valid.
  4. Use our eligibility quiz and document checklist to rebuild a gap list.
  5. Book a free consult on WhatsApp +92 304 5999859 with the refusal attached.

When re-applying is a bad idea

  • The programme was never a realistic academic fit
  • Funds still cannot be evidenced lawfully
  • Someone asks you to hide the refusal or fabricate papers
