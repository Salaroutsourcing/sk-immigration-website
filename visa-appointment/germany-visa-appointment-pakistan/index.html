Frequently asked questions

How do I book a Germany visa appointment from Pakistan?

Complete the online application for your visa category, pay the required fees, then schedule biometrics/interview via the official VFS, TLS, UKVI, US consular or IRCC channel for that country. SK Immigration helps you sequence documents before locking a date.

Should I book the appointment before documents are ready?

Usually no. Peak slots create pressure, but attending with an incomplete file risks refusal or delay. We aim for 'ready file first, then book'.

What documents are commonly needed for visa appointments?

Passport, application confirmation, fee receipts, photos, financial evidence, purpose documents (admission, invitation, employment), and category-specific forms. Exact lists depend on the destination.

Can SK Immigration get a 'special slot'?
