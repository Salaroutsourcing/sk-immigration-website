Why Pakistani students choose Germany

Public universities often charge low or no tuition for degree programmes, while living costs are planned through a blocked account (Sperrkonto). Many applicants also compare full degrees with Ausbildung (earn-while-you-train). The right path depends on marks, language and career goal — not agent promises.

Study vs Ausbildung — pick honestly

  • Degree / Master: admission letter, funding proof, often English or German programme rules
  • Ausbildung: training contract + German language (often A2–B1+, higher for nursing)
  • Red flag: anyone selling a “guaranteed German visa” without a real university or employer

Requirements (Pakistan applicants)

  • University admission or Ausbildung contract from a recognized German institution
  • Proof of funds (blocked account amount changes — verify current embassy guidance)
  • Passport valid 6+ months, biometric photos, completed national visa forms
  • Academic transcripts and certificates (attestation / APS where your case requires it)
  • Health insurance meeting German entry rules
  • Language proof matching the programme or employer (German and/or English)

Step-by-step process from Pakistan

  1. Free profile review — study vs Ausbildung, budget and language reality
  2. Shortlist universities or Ausbildung employers; prepare CV / motivation letter
  3. Secure admission or training contract
  4. Open / confirm blocked account or other accepted funding proof
  5. Package documents, book appointment, attend biometrics / interview
  6. Track decision and prepare travel / enrolment

Documents checklist

  • Passport and photos

  • Academic certificates and transcripts

Frequently asked questions

What are the main requirements for a Germany study visa from Pakistan?

Admission or Ausbildung contract, funding proof (often blocked account), passport, academics, insurance, language evidence matching your pathway, and APS/attestation when your case requires it. SK Immigration builds a case-specific checklist.

Do Pakistani students need APS for Germany?

Many degree pathways require APS certificate review. Confirm current APS Pakistan rules for your program type before you open a blocked account or book VFS. APS queues are outside any consultant’s control.

How much blocked account do I need?

The required amount changes — always verify current embassy guidance before opening an account. Ausbildung applicants usually show salary via the training contract instead of a classic student Sperrkonto.

How long does a Germany student visa take from Pakistan?

Often 4–7 months from shortlist to decision. APS queues, language gaps, peak winter intakes and incomplete funding proofs take longer.

Do I need German language for a degree taught in English?

Not always for admission, but daily life and part-time work are easier with German. Ausbildung almost always needs German (often A2–B1+, higher for nursing).
