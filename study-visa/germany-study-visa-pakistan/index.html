Why Pakistani students choose Germany

Public universities often charge low or no tuition for degree programmes, while living costs are planned through a blocked account (Sperrkonto). Many applicants also compare full degrees with Ausbildung (earn-while-you-train). The right path depends on marks, language and career goal — not agent promises.

Study vs Ausbildung — pick honestly

  • Degree / Master: admission letter, funding proof, often English or German programme rules
  • Ausbildung: training contract + German language (often A2–B1+, higher for nursing)
  • Red flag: anyone selling a “guaranteed German visa” without a real university or employer

Requirements (Pakistan applicants)

  • University admission or Ausbildung contract from a recognized German institution
  • Proof of funds (blocked account amount changes — verify current embassy guidance)
  • Passport valid 6+ months, biometric photos, completed national visa forms
  • Academic transcripts and certificates (attestation / APS where your case requires it)
  • Health insurance meeting German entry rules
  • Language proof matching the programme or employer (German and/or English)

APS for Pakistani applicants (do not skip this step)

For many degree pathways, German universities and missions expect an APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) review of Pakistani school/university credentials. APS is not a visa, and it is not optional “if your agent is confident.” Treat it as a sequencing problem:

  1. Confirm whether your program type and university still require APS (rules and product lines change — check the current APS Pakistan / German mission pages).
  2. Prepare attested academics, transcripts and identity papers in the order APS asks for — not a random WhatsApp checklist.
  3. Do not open a blocked account or buy a peak-season VFS slot until you know whether APS is blocking your admission or visa file.
  4. Keep APS receipts and certificate copies with the same name spellings as your passport and admission letter.

Blocked account (Sperrkonto) — Pakistan planning notes

Most classic student residence cases show living funds via a German blocked account. The amount is not a permanent number — German authorities update it. Before you transfer money:

  • Read the current figure on Make-it-in-Germany / German mission guidance (not a 2022 Facebook screenshot).
  • Use a provider the mission accepts for your visa category.
  • Match the account holder name to the passport used on the visa form.
  • Ausbildung applicants usually prove funds through the training salary, not a full student Sperrkonto — mixing the two categories is a common refusal trigger.

Living-cost reality (planning ranges — verify before you transfer)

Public tuition can be low or semester-contribution only, but living costs are real. Many students plan roughly €850–€1,200 / month depending on city (Munich and Hamburg differ from smaller university towns). Your blocked-account requirement is designed to cover a year of living funds at the official rate — it is not “extra pocket money.” Rent deposits, health insurance and transport cards sit on top of day-to-day food. We help you model a Pakistan-family budget before you commit to a city.

After the visa — post-study work context

Germany’s appeal for Pakistani graduates is partly the chance to stay and seek skilled work after an eligible degree (job-seeker / transition rules apply and change — confirm current residence-law guidance). That is not a promise of a job offer, and it is not a reason to invent a fake “work visa inside a student file.” Study intent must be genuine. If your real goal is immediate full-time work without a training contract or degree, talk to us about Germany work permit or Ausbildung instead of forcing a student narrative.

Pakistan-specific document traps (Germany)
