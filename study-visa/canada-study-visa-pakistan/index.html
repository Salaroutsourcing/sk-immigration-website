Canada study permit from Pakistan — what actually matters

IRCC looks at programme fit, funds, and a coherent study plan — not agent slogans. You need a letter of acceptance from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI). Some intakes also need a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL). SK Immigration builds the narrative and checklist; IRCC decides every study permit.

Requirements

  • Letter of acceptance from a DLI (and PAL when your programme/intake requires it)
  • Proof of funds covering tuition + living (GIC and/or bank evidence as applicable)
  • Study plan / SOP that matches your academics and career logic
  • Biometrics for Pakistani applicants; medicals when required
  • Passport and complete IRCC online forms
