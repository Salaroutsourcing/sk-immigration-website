How the UK Student route works from Pakistan

UK study is CAS-first: you need an offer from a licensed Student sponsor, meet conditions (often a deposit), receive a CAS, then apply on GOV.UK with funds, English and TB evidence as required. SK Immigration coaches the sequence — UKVI decides.

Requirements Pakistani applicants must clear

  • CAS from a licensed UK sponsor institution
  • Tuition + living-cost funds in the format UKVI expects (seasoning rules matter)
  • English language evidence (approved Secure English Language Test unless exempt)
  • TB test for Pakistan residents when required for the route
  • Valid passport and accurate online application matching the CAS

Process we run with clients

  1. Budget and profile review — UK vs Europe alternatives if funds are tight
  2. University shortlist and offer strategy
  3. CAS readiness checklist (conditions, deposit, documents)
  4. Funds documentation / maintenance evidence
  5. GOV.UK application + VAC biometrics
  6. Decision tracking, IHS understanding and travel planning

Documents

  • CAS letter with correct personal details
  • Passport
  • Bank statements / financial evidence meeting UKVI format
  • English test results (accepted type and score)
  • TB certificate when required
  • Academic transcripts and prior study evidence

Common UK refusal / delay triggers

  • Funds not held long enough or wrong account format
  • CAS details that do not match the online form
  • Unaccepted English test type or expired scores
  • Skipping TB when required
  • Credibility gaps (programme does not fit academic history)

UK Student visa from Pakistan — CAS, maintenance, credibility

A UK Student visa file is built around a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed sponsor, maintenance funds rules, and a credible study story. SK Immigration prepares packaging and interview readiness; UKVI decides.

Core building blocks

  • Offer + CAS from a licensed student sponsor
  • Tuition and maintenance funds held correctly for the required period (verify current UKVI guidance — do not rely on WhatsApp screenshots)
  • English language evidence meeting the course / UKVI route rules
  • TB test and other biometrics / identity steps when required for Pakistan applicants

Process map

  1. University shortlist and application
  2. Offer conditions cleared → CAS
  3. Funds timing and document packaging
  4. Online application + biometrics
  5. Decision and travel readiness

IELTS / English

Many courses require Secure English Language Tests at stated levels. Some applicants may meet English another way under UKVI/university rules — confirm on the CAS/offer, not from generic blogs.

High-risk mistakes

  • Depositing funds too late or moving money in unexplained lumps
  • Course hopping without academic logic
  • Using an unlicensed “sponsor” story
  • Treating visit visas as a backdoor to study a full degree

Frequently asked questions

What is a CAS and when do I get it?

CAS means Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies from a licensed sponsor after you meet offer conditions (often including a deposit). You need it before applying for the Student visa.

How much funds do I need for a UK student visa from Pakistan?

Enough to cover remaining tuition (as shown on CAS) plus UKVI maintenance amounts for your location — held in the accepted format for the required period. We review statements case by case.

Is IELTS mandatory?

Usually an approved Secure English Language Test is required unless you qualify for an exemption. MOI letters alone are rarely enough for UKVI Student route.

How long does UK student visa take from Pakistan?

Often weeks after biometrics once CAS is ready; overall planning commonly 2–5 months including offers and funds seasoning.
